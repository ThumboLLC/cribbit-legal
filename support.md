Cribbit Support
===============

Cribbit is published by Thumbo LLC. If something isn't working, or you have a question or an idea, email us:

**dev@thumbo.com**

We read every message. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version, and tell us what happened and what you expected. Please don't send recordings or private notes.

## Common questions

**How do I cancel my voice subscription?**
Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Cribbit Voice. You can also open Cribbit, go to Settings (the gear icon on Home), and tap "Manage voice subscription." Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid being charged for the next one. Writing, tasks and organizing in Cribbit stay free.

**I subscribed, but voice isn't working.**
In Cribbit, go to Settings and tap "Restore purchases," then tap "Restore purchases" again on the screen that opens. This checks your Apple ID for an active subscription. If it still doesn't work, email us.

**How do I get a refund?**
Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at https://reportaproblem.apple.com.

**What happens to my notes if I delete the app?**
Your notes are stored on your phone. If you delete Cribbit, they may be removed with it, unless your phone backup restores them. Copy anything important out of the app first.

**Where does my audio go?**
When you use voice, your audio is sent securely to a transcription service only to turn it into text. Cribbit doesn't keep it. See our Privacy Policy for details: https://thumbollc.github.io/cribbit-legal/

**Do I need an account?**
No. Cribbit doesn't have accounts or logins.
